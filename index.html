01 / A QUESTION

Could you imagine going a day without reliable access to safe drinking water?

Take a moment. Then choose the answer that reflects your experience.

There is no right or wrong answer.

02 / THE REGIONAL PICTURE · HERAT, 2023

44%

of respondents in an IRC assessment reported losing access to water points after the earthquake.

ACAPS, Earthquakes in Herat province, 12 October 2023, citing an IRC assessment conducted on 9 October 2023.

This is an assessment finding, not a percentage of the entire population of Herat.

03 / THE SCALE OF DAMAGE · WESTERN AFGHANISTAN

258

water systems were destroyed across the western province following the October 2023 earthquakes.

UNICEF Afghanistan, 22 March 2024.

The impact was not limited to one household or one village. Damage to water infrastructure affected families and children across the earthquake-affected region.

04 / ZINDAJAN · THE CHILDREN

244 / 380

children were among the survivors reported at Gazarga transit centre in Herat City.

WHO, Afghanistan Earthquake Health Situation Report No. 4, October 2023.

This describes the group reported at the transit centre. It is not the total number of displaced children in Zindajan.

Approximately 64.2% of this reported group were children.

05 / THE REAL ISSUE

Water is essential. So are healthcare, education and protection.

When displacement interrupts access to these essentials, children face more than the immediate effects of an earthquake. They face unequal opportunities to recover and rebuild their lives.

Displacement should change a child's location — not their access to opportunity.

06 / THREE POLICY DIRECTIONS

01

Long-Term Recovery & Family Assistance

Connect displaced families with continued support beyond the immediate emergency response.

02

Mobile Healthcare

Bring essential healthcare, referrals and child-sensitive support closer to underserved communities.

03

Mobility-Adaptive Education

Help children continue learning after displacement through flexible enrolment and a proposed Portable Learning Identity.

A student policy proposal under SDG 10.2 and 10.3.
